### Day-by-Day Itinerary

#### Day 1: Arrival & The Pink City Heart (21 September 2026)
* **Morning:** Catch an early morning train or AC bus from Delhi to Jaipur (approx. 5 hours). Check into your hotel in central Jaipur/C-Scheme.
* **Afternoon:** Head straight to the **City Palace** to explore its courtyards, museums, and royal courtyards, followed by a quick walk past **Hawa Mahal** (Palace of Winds).
* **Evening:** Stroll through the bustling Johari Bazaar. Stop by **Shrinat Lassiwala** on MI Road for a rich, traditional kulhad lassi, and enjoy dinner at a rooftop spot like **Peacock Rooftop Restaurant** for classic Rajasthani dal bati churma or local tandoori dishes.

#### Day 2: The Mighty Forts of Jaipur (22 September 2026)
* **Morning:** Take an auto-rickshaw or cab up to the breathtaking **Amber Fort** (UNESCO World Heritage site). Explore the stunning *Sheesh Mahal* (Mirror Palace), Diwan-i-Am, and Dalaram Bagh. *(Tip: Walk or take a jeep up; avoid elephant rides for animal welfare reasons).*
* **Afternoon:** Continue up the ridge to explore **Jaigarh Fort** (home to Jaivana, the world's largest wheeled cannon) and the panoramic hilltop views at **Nahargarh Fort**.
* **Evening:** Watch a gorgeous sunset over the entire Pink City from the ramparts of Nahargarh Fort. Head back down to the city for a comforting dinner of local specialities like laal mass or vegetarian gatte ki sabzi.

#### Day 3: Temples, Markets & Return to Delhi (23 September 2026)
* **Morning:** Visit the ornate **Birla Mandir** or the peaceful **Govind Dev Ji Temple** near City Palace, then pick up local handicrafts, blue pottery, or block-printed textiles in the walled city.
* **Afternoon:** Grab an early lunch featuring street snacks and sweets (try samosas and mithai at **Gangaur Sweets**), then head to the railway station or bus stand for your journey back to Delhi.
* **Evening:** Arrive back in Delhi.
