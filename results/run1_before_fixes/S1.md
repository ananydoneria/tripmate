A royal 3-day getaway from Delhi to the Pink City of Jaipur for 2 people, focused on historic forts and delectable Rajasthani cuisine, staying comfortably within your ₹30,000 budget.

### Day-by-Day Plan

#### Day 1: Royal Palaces & Sunset Views
*   **Morning**: Take the early morning Ajmer Shatabdi Express from Delhi (approx. 6:10 AM – 10:40 AM). Check into a charming mid-range heritage hotel (such as *Hotel Pearl Palace* or *Umaid Bhawan*).
*   **Afternoon**: Head into the Old City for a traditional Rajasthani lunch at the iconic **LMB (Laxmi Mishthan Bhandar)**. Afterward, visit the stunning **Hawa Mahal** (Palace of Winds) and the adjacent **City Palace** to marvel at the royal courtyards and museum collections.
*   **Evening**: Catch an auto-rickshaw or taxi up to **Nahargarh Fort** (Tiger Fort) for a spectacular sunset view over the Pink City. Enjoy dinner at *Peacock Rooftop Restaurant* or at the fort's terrace restaurant.

#### Day 2: Grand Forts & Cultural Feast
*   **Morning**: Beat the heat and head north early (around 8:30 AM) to the magnificent **Amber Fort** (Indian entry is ₹100). Wander through the mirror-adorned *Sheesh Mahal* (Glass Palace). Skip the elephant rides due to animal welfare concerns and enjoy the walk or take a jeep. Continue uphill to **Jaigarh Fort** to view *Jaivana*, once the world's largest cannon on wheels.
*   **Afternoon**: Stop by the picturesque **Jal Mahal** (Water Palace) for photos on your way back. For a late lunch, indulge in the legendary, spicy *Pyaz Kachoris* at **Rawat Mishthan Bhandar** and grab a thick, sweet clay-pot lassi at the famous **Lassiwala** on MI Road.
*   **Evening**: Travel to **Chokhi Dhani**, an ethnic Rajasthani village resort. Experience lively folk dances, puppet shows, and camel rides before enjoying an unlimited, authentic *Dal Baati Churma* feast served traditionally on leaf platters.

#### Day 3: Science, Souvenirs & Sweet Treats
*   **Morning**: Visit **Jantar Mantar**, a fascinating UNESCO World Heritage site featuring giant, precise astronomical stone instruments and the world's largest stone sundial.
*   **Afternoon**: Spend your final hours shopping for local handicrafts, block-print textiles, and jewelry at the vibrant **Johari Bazaar** and **Bapu Bazaar**. Stop by *Gangaur Sweets* for a quick bite and pack some fresh *Ghevar* (sweet) to take back to Delhi.
*   **Evening**: Board the return Shatabdi or Double Decker train back to Delhi, arriving by night.
