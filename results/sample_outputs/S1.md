### Day-by-Day Itinerary

- **Evening:** Stroll through the bustling bazaars of the Walled City and stop by **Shrinat Lassiwala** on MI Road for a traditional thick lassi, followed by dinner at a rooftop spot like **Peacock Rooftop Restaurant**.

#### Day 2: Majestic Forts of Jaipur (22 September 2026)
- **Morning:** Visit the magnificent **Amber Fort** (explore the Sheesh Mahal and historical courtyards; opt to walk or take a jeep rather than elephant rides).
- **Afternoon:** Continue up to **Jaigarh Fort** to view the giant Jaivana cannon and take in panoramic views, then stop by the picturesque **Nahargarh Fort** for sunset.
- **Evening:** Indulge in authentic Rajasthani local food (dal bati churma) at a classic city restaurant.

#### Day 3: Heritage & Return to Delhi (23 September 2026)
- **Morning:** Visit the unique stone astronomical observatory at **Jantar Mantar**, then pick up some local sweets at **Gangaur Sweets**.
- **Afternoon:** Have an early lunch, check out of your hotel, and board your return transport to Delhi.
